Planted faults

Broken variants the checker must reject, injected at three layers: wrong input values, bugs in the generator's maths, and broken exported solids. Train faults are shown to the agents that write rules, mined faults come from datasheets and IP67 / drop-test practice, and held-out faults were written blind by a separate agent and never shown to the rule writers, so they measure whether the rules generalise.
